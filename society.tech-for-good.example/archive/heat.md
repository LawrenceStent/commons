# Heat and people living alone (notes for this example)

These are illustrative notes written for the example society, not research. Replace them with your own sources.

Older people, people with long-term illness and people living alone are usually named as most at risk in hot
weather. Advice commonly given: keep curtains closed on the sunny side, drink regularly, and check on people who
live alone during a heatwave.

Check-in schemes rely on a phone call or a visit at a set time. Their weak points are volunteers dropping out
and people who don't answer the phone; schemes usually agree in advance what to do when someone can't be reached.
