# Repair days for mobility aids (notes for this example)

These are illustrative notes written for the example society, not research. Replace them with your own sources.

Community repair events usually fix small faults on the spot: loose brakes on walking frames, worn ferrules
(rubber tips) on sticks, flat or worn wheelchair tyres, loose armrests and footplates. Anything structural,
electrical or on a powered chair is normally referred to the official service or the supplier.

Groups that run repair events tend to keep a short intake form: the owner's consent, what is wrong, what was
done, and a safety check before the aid is handed back. Insurance and who is liable if a repair fails are the
questions volunteers most often raise first.
