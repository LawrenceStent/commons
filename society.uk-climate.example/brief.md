Investigate climate and environmental questions about the United Kingdom from public records: what government
bodies, regulators, companies and infrastructure operators have done, reported and been found to have done.
Prefer primary sources: GOV.UK publications, Environment Agency data, Ofwat and Ofgem decisions, Climate Change
Committee reports, Hansard and legislation. Use news and NGO reports to find leads, then check them against the
primary record. Say plainly what the record doesn't show. Organisations, events, infrastructure and public records
only: never individuals.
